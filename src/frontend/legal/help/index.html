Getting Started

What file types can I upload? expand_more

ConfigGuard accepts .txt, .cfg, .conf, and .xml files up to 10MB each, and supports uploading multiple files at once. These should be raw configuration exports from your device (e.g. "show running-config" output on Cisco, or a Junos/PAN-OS config export).

Do I need to tell ConfigGuard which vendor my device is? expand_more

No — vendor selection is optional. ConfigGuard will attempt to auto-detect your vendor (Cisco IOS, Juniper Junos, Palo Alto PAN-OS) from the config's syntax. If confidence is low, you'll be shown a confirmation screen with its best guess and the reason for it, so you can confirm or correct it.

What happens if my vendor isn't recognized at all? expand_more

If no known vendor signature matches, ConfigGuard flags the config for AI-assisted review. Unrecognized lines are shown to you with an AI-suggested category, which you confirm or correct in the "Review Unknown Lines" screen before a report is generated.

Why is a field in my report showing "Not Evaluated" instead of Pass/Fail? expand_more

"Not Evaluated" means the converter couldn't determine that value from your config file — it's different from a pass or fail, and means that control simply couldn't be automatically checked. It's shown so nothing is silently skipped from your report.

Understanding Your Report

Which compliance frameworks does ConfigGuard check against? expand_more

CIS Benchmarks, NIST 800-53, DISA STIG, and India-specific frameworks (RBI Cyber Security Framework, SEBI Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework). RBI/SEBI checks are our own technical interpretation of those frameworks' general principles for device-level config settings — not an official or certified regulatory mapping.

Are the remediation CLI commands safe to run directly on my device? expand_more

Remediation commands are sourced directly from our rule pack (never generated or altered by AI), but you should always review and test any change in a non-production or lab environment before applying it to a live device. ConfigGuard cannot account for your specific network topology or dependencies.

Can I download my report as a PDF? expand_more

Yes — the compliance report dashboard provides a downloadable PDF containing device identification, the findings table, and full remediation paths.

Data & Privacy

Is my uploaded config file sent to an external AI? expand_more

Only unrecognized individual lines — not your whole file — may be sent to a third-party AI provider to suggest a category, and a filtering step is applied first to withhold known-sensitive values like passwords or keys. Every AI suggestion requires your manual confirmation before it's used. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How long is my data stored? expand_more

Uploaded files and reports are stored to power your scan history for as long as this prototype is being evaluated. You can request deletion at any time — see below.

How do I request deletion of my data? expand_more

Contact the project team using the details below with the scan ID or filename you'd like removed, and we'll delete the associated files, reports, and any confirmed line mappings tied to it.
