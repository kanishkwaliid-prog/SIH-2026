2. What ConfigGuard Does

ConfigGuard accepts network device configuration files (e.g. Cisco IOS, Juniper Junos, Palo Alto PAN-OS) that you upload, converts them into a normalized format, evaluates them against security benchmarks (including CIS, NIST, STIG, and India-specific frameworks such as RBI and SEBI guidance), and generates a compliance report with suggested remediation.

3. Your Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for redacting or anonymizing any data in a config file you are not authorized to expose to a third-party tool, including this one.
  • You will not use the Service to test, probe, or gather information about systems without authorization from their owner.
  • Findings and remediation commands are suggestions only — you are responsible for reviewing and testing any change before applying it to a real device.

5. AI-Assisted Analysis

For configuration lines the Service cannot classify automatically, ConfigGuard may send a limited, filtered excerpt to a third-party AI provider to suggest a likely category. A safety filter is applied before anything is sent, intended to withhold known-sensitive values (such as passwords, keys, or credentials) from that request. No AI suggestion is applied automatically — every AI-suggested classification requires your explicit human confirmation before it is used in a report.
