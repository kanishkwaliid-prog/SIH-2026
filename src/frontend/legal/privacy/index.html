Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]  •  Applies to the ConfigGuard hackathon prototype (SIH 2026)

warning

Network configuration files can contain sensitive infrastructure details — internal IP addresses, hostnames, hashed or weakly-encrypted passwords, and SNMP strings. Please only upload files you are authorized to share, and remove anything you would not want stored on a third-party server.

1. What We Collect

  • Uploaded configuration files — the raw text of any file you submit for analysis.
  • Generated reports and findings — compliance results, severity levels, and the vendor/device metadata derived from your upload.
  • Human review confirmations — vendor selections and unknown-line category confirmations you provide.
  • Basic usage data — timestamps of scans, for the purpose of populating your scan history dashboard.

2. How We Store It

Uploaded files and reports are sent to and stored on our backend server so that they can appear in your scan history and results dashboard. This is a hackathon-stage prototype: our storage has not undergone a formal security audit or penetration test, and should not be treated as suitable for storing production-sensitive network data long-term.

3. AI-Assisted Processing (Unknown Vendor / Unknown Line Path)

When ConfigGuard encounters a configuration line it cannot classify using its built-in rule-based parsers, it may send that line to a third-party AI provider to suggest a likely category (e.g. "this looks like an SSH timeout setting"). Before this happens:

  • A filtering step is applied that is designed to withhold known-sensitive values — such as passwords, secrets, keys, or credential strings — from what is sent to the external AI provider.
  • Only the minimal line or pattern needed for classification is sent, not your entire configuration file.
  • No AI-suggested category is applied automatically. A human must review and confirm or correct every AI suggestion before it is used in your final report.

No filtering system is infallible. If a config line itself contains sensitive data embedded in an unusual or non-standard format, avoid uploading it, or manually redact it before uploading.

4. Confirmed Answers & Learning Memory

When you confirm or correct an AI-suggested category for an unrecognized config line, that vendor/pattern/category mapping is saved so that ConfigGuard can recognize the same pattern automatically in future scans, without needing to ask again or re-query the AI provider.

5. Who Can Access Your Data

Data you upload is accessible to the ConfigGuard project team for the purposes of running, debugging, and demonstrating this hackathon prototype. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Data sent to the third-party AI provider for line classification is subject to that provider's own data handling terms in addition to ours.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Uploaded files and reports are retained to support your scan history for as long as this prototype is being evaluated. You can request deletion of your uploaded files, reports, and confirmed mappings at any time via the Help Centre.

7. Your Choices

  • You may redact or anonymize IP addresses, hostnames, and identifying details in a config file before uploading it.
  • You may request a copy or deletion of any data associated with your scans.
  • You may choose not to use the Service for any configuration file you are not comfortable sharing under these terms.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the prototype evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or concerns, reach out via the Help Centre.
